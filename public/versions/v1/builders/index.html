STUDENT LED. BUILT TO BUILD.

More momentum.
More makers.
More possibility.

Builders Collective brings students together to make things and grow through the national hackathon circuit.

Penn State Engineering covered the community’s formation in February 2026, alongside the c0mpiled-6/pennstate sprint hackathon. Organizers Het Sheth and Ishaan Narang worked with support from Ted Graef and Brad Groznik.

Public student posts have since celebrated the club’s launch, a mini-hackathon with Vercel, and participation at Bitcamp 2026.
