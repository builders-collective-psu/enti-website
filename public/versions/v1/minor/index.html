GOOD TO KNOW

A clear path
from here.

What grades do I need?+

Earn a C or better in every course that counts toward the minor.

Can courses count toward my major too?+

At least six credits in the minor must be distinct from the prescribed courses required by your major. Ask your adviser how your specific courses fit.

Can I count an internship or research?+

With an adviser’s approval, up to three credits of relevant research, internship, or independent study may replace coursework within a cluster.

How do I add the minor?+

Discuss your plan with your major adviser and the adviser for your chosen ENTI cluster. Use LionPATH’s minor declaration process when you’re ready. Open LionPATH+

Do I need to have a startup idea?+

You can use entrepreneurial skills to launch a venture, work in an existing organization, or create change in your community. You can begin with a question or an interest.

Where can I see the complete requirements?+

Your applicable Bulletin and academic adviser guide your degree plan. Read the full ENTI Bulletin entry+
